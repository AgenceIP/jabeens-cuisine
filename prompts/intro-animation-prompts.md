## DOCUMENT 2 — SEEDANCE 2.0: STORYBOARDED CLIP SEQUENCE

### ACT 1 (0–5 seconds)

---

#### CLIP 1 — The Reveal

**Seed image:** PROMPT A hero image (Beef Seekh Kebab — recommended for maximum textural drama in macro)

---

##### SCENE

Opening frame: extreme close-up of the beef seekh kebab char surface. The camera is so close that the entire 16:9 frame is occupied by approximately 4cm of kebab surface — char ridges read as mountain topography, coriander seed halves appear as boulders. The context of what this is remains completely unreadable for the first 1.5 seconds. The subject could be volcanic rock, ancient leather, a meteorite surface. There is nothing in frame except texture and the light that rakes across it. The platter below the kebab is not yet visible. The void surrounding the food is absolute. This is not food photography — it is geological survey of a surface that happens to be edible.

---

##### CAMERA

- **Opening position:** Approximately 8mm from kebab surface, lens axis perpendicular to the char face. Equivalent focal length at this distance: extreme macro, approximately 1:1 reproduction ratio.
- **Movement:** A smooth, continuous orbital pull-back. The camera retreats on a curved path — not a straight zoom out, not a dolly back on a straight axis. The path curves slightly upward (approximately 15 degrees of elevation gain over the full 5 seconds) while simultaneously rotating 8 degrees clockwise around the food's vertical axis. By the end of the clip, viewpoint is at a low 3/4 angle (approximately 35 degrees elevation, 20 degrees off the food's centerline).
- **Speed profile:** Starts at near-zero movement speed for the first 0.5 seconds. Accelerates gently through seconds 1–3 using ease-in curve. Holds steady pull-back speed seconds 3–4.5. Decelerates to a gentle stop at second 5 with ease-out curve. Total distance traveled: approximately equivalent to pulling from 8mm to 400mm from the subject.
- **No shake, no handheld:** Movement is entirely mechanical — locked-off fluid head motion. Precise, indifferent, observational.

---

##### ACTION ARC

- **0:00–0:05:** Complete stillness. Frame full of char texture. Light rakes hard from camera-left. Single tendril of steam rises through the upper-right of the frame, its source below frame.
- **0:05–1:30:** Camera begins retreating. Char texture slowly resolves — individual char ridges begin to be recognizable. The steam tendril's source starts to enter frame from the bottom.
- **1:30–2:30:** The kebab's cylindrical form becomes readable. Still extreme close — but now clearly food. The iron platter surface appears at the lower edge of frame.
- **2:30–4:00:** Continued retreat. The full kebab is now in frame. The platter extends to the frame edges. Steam rises steadily. Black void on all sides beyond the platter edge.
- **4:00–5:00:** Camera decelerates to its stop position. Platter centered in frame, kebab resting within it, void surrounding completely.

---

##### TEXT CHOREOGRAPHY

No text. No UI. Background plate only.

React overlay layer should have zero active elements during Clip 1 (0–5 seconds).

---

##### LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE

- **Source:** Single hard light, camera-left at 35 degrees horizontal from lens axis, elevated 12 degrees above the food's horizon plane. 3200K tungsten equivalent.
- **Behavior across clip:** Because the camera is moving, the light's raking angle relative to the visible food surface changes. As the camera pulls back and elevates, the same light source begins to catch the steam wisps — they become visible as luminous threads approximately 2.5 seconds in, as the angle between camera and light becomes favorable for backscatter.
- **Shadows:** Hard-edged. Every surface irregularity casts a shadow proportional to its height relative to the light.
- **Absolute black:** No ambient fill at any point. Shadow side of the kebab, the platter beyond the light's reach, the entire void — all render at #000000.

---

##### ENTRY / EXIT STATES

**Entry state:** Pure texture fill — char surface at extreme macro, no readable context, single steam tendril entering top-right, hard raking light from left. Movement: zero.

**Exit state:** Full kebab on platter, centered in frame, low 3/4 angle view (35 degrees elevation), void surrounding completely. Steam rising steadily. Camera fully stopped. Export this frame as a still — it becomes the seed image for Clip 2.

---

##### TECHNICAL

- Duration: 5.0 seconds
- Resolution: 3840x2160 preferred (4K)
- Frame rate: 60fps
- Seed image: PROMPT A hero image, cropped to emphasize the char surface center section
- Motion model: Camera pull-back is an arc, not a zoom. Focal length remains approximately constant while physical camera-to-subject distance increases. Bezier curve interpolation — not linear.
- Output codec: H.264 or H.265, black level at 16 (broadcast black)
- Export last frame as still → seed image for Clip 2

### ACT 2 (5–10 seconds)

---

#### CLIP 2 — The Deconstruction

**Seed image:** Last frame still export from Clip 1

---

##### SCENE

The same composition that ended Clip 1 — dish on platter in void — is the opening frame. Over 5 seconds, the dish undergoes a gravitationally impossible but physically plausible-feeling deconstruction. Individual components separate and begin to orbit outward: spice particles (ground cumin, coriander, paprika), fragments of char crust, rendered fat droplets (amber-gold, spherical under surface tension), wisps of steam that are now semi-solid-feeling. These components drift outward at varying speeds and trajectories, each following a slow orbital arc rather than exploding outward. The platter remains in frame throughout, first losing the kebab (which breaks apart slowly), then losing the platter's carbonized grease film (which peels away and joins the orbit). By second 9, the platter is bare and the void contains a slow constellation of food matter in weightless drift. The physics feel closer to deep space object behavior than kitchen physics — but the materials are entirely culinary.

---

##### CAMERA

- **Opening position:** Identical to Clip 1 exit state — low 3/4 angle, 35 degrees elevation, 20 degrees off-axis. This continuity is mandatory.
- **0:00–1:30 (clip time):** Camera holds its position completely. The deconstruction is happening to the dish, not because of camera movement. The world is changing; the observer is not.
- **1:30–4:00:** Very slow, barely perceptible dolly backward — approximately equivalent to adding 30mm of distance over 2.5 seconds. This creates additional frame space at the periphery for orbiting ingredients.
- **4:00–5:00:** Camera holds. Frame is a still composition of bare platter at center and ingredient constellation distributed around it.
- **No rotation during Clip 2.** Camera's positional drift is purely translational — backward on the lens axis.

---

##### ACTION ARC

- **0:00–1:00:** Nothing moves except steam, which continues its upward drift from the kebab surface. A 1-second held breath — the audience reads the composition before the world changes.
- **1:00–1:30:** First separation: a section of char crust lifts approximately 3mm from the kebab's exterior — releases as if pressure differential has equalized. Drifts upward and slightly to camera-left at approximately 0.4mm/s. Individual coriander seed half remains attached to this fragment.
- **1:30–2:30:** Kebab's meat interior begins to separate from the crust in additional zones — not all at once, not symmetrically. Three or four separation events, each unique in trajectory. Rendered fat beads, released from interior pockets, become spherical and drift in different directions.
- **2:30–3:30:** The platter itself begins to shed its carbonized surface layer — thin dark flakes lift and join the outer orbit. The platter below is revealed as raw iron. The drift field now occupies most of the mid-ground.
- **3:30–4:30:** All primary separation complete. The platter is bare iron in void. The ingredient constellation fills the frame's depth — items at various distances from camera, all in extremely slow drift. No two items move at the same speed or on the same vector.
- **4:30–5:00:** Everything decelerates toward stillness. At second 5, all elements have reached near-zero velocity. The frame is a static composition of dispersed ingredients in void.

---

##### TEXT CHOREOGRAPHY

No text. No UI. Background plate only.

React overlay remains inactive (0 opacity on all text elements) through the end of second 10 (overall timeline).

---

##### LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE

- **Source: same single light, same position as Clip 1** — critical for continuity. The light does not move.
- As ingredients separate and drift into three-dimensional space, items drifting into the light's direct path become brighter; items drifting into the shadow zone become progressively darker, eventually absorbed into the void.
- **Fat droplets:** Being spherical and semi-translucent, they render as small amber lanterns — light passes through them, creating a warm gold glow specific to this ingredient type.
- **Steam wisps:** Slow their rise, flatten, and eventually disperse as the heat source deconstructs. By second 8, no new steam. Last wisps dissolve at approximately second 9.
- **Atmosphere:** As ingredients drift outward, the void between them and the camera becomes more present. The black is not empty — it is the main character of the second half of this clip.

---

##### ENTRY / EXIT STATES

**Entry state:** Identical to Clip 1 exit — kebab on platter, centered, low 3/4 angle, steam active, void surrounding. Full continuity.

**Exit state:** Bare iron platter at frame center. Constellation of separated ingredients distributed at varying depths and lateral positions throughout the void. All elements near-stationary. This frame is a spatial map that Clips 3A/3B/3C will interrupt as a hard editorial cut.

**Export last frame as still → seed image for Clip 4.**

---

##### TECHNICAL

- Duration: 5.0 seconds
- Resolution: 3840x2160
- Frame rate: 60fps
- Seed image: Last frame still export from Clip 1
- Physics guidance: Each separating element should be given a unique initial velocity vector with a slight deceleration curve. No two elements should share a trajectory. Char fragments rotate slowly on their dominant axis during drift (0.5–1.0 RPM equivalent). Fat spheres do not rotate but oscillate very slightly in shape (surface tension behavior).
- Depth of field behavior: items drifting toward camera enter near focus zone and defocus forward. Items drifting away blur into background bokeh.
- **Hard cut out:** Clip 2 ends on a hard editorial cut to Clip 3A. No transition. No dissolve. The contrast between the floaty void of Clip 2 and the intense heat of Clip 3A is the edit.

### ACT 4 (13–15 seconds)

---

#### CLIP 4 — The Convergence

**Seed image:** Last frame still export from Clip 2 — the ingredient constellation (char fragments, fat spheres, spice particles, steam wisps dispersed in void around bare iron platter)

---

##### SCENE

The void from Act 2 — the ingredient constellation floating in darkness around the bare platter — is the opening state. For 2 seconds, the constellation is held in near-stasis. Then, over the next 1.5 to 2 seconds, every particle in the void begins to move inward. Not chaotically — each element traces an arc back toward the platter's center. The physics suggest that a gravitational mass has activated at the platter's center point. This is not the deconstruction reversed in reverse playback — each element finds its own unique inward arc, not the exact reversal of its outward trajectory. The convergence is slightly slower than the deconstruction was, and trajectories curve more — as if each ingredient is in a low orbit that is decaying inward. As items return to the center, they do not re-form the kebab. They simply arrive at the center and are absorbed into the dark, one by one.
